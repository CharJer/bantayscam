## 🛡️ Project Overview
**BantayScam** is an intelligent, decentralized mobile security companion engineered to protect Filipino consumers, elderly family members, and micro-merchants from the rampant epidemic of smishing, fake e-wallet notifications (GCash/Maya spoofing), Facebook Marketplace courier fraud, and unauthorized SIM-swap attacks.

---

## ⚡ Emerging Technologies Integrated

### 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **On-Device Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Real-time lexical and semantic analysis of incoming SMS notifications to flag deceptive urgency, spoofed alphanumeric sender IDs, and credential-harvesting narratives.
- **Multimodal Computer Vision OCR**: Automated visual layout and typeface verification for transaction screenshots, detecting edited reference numbers, font mismatches, and tampered e-wallet receipts.
- **BantayAI Conversational Threat Assistant**: Explains complex security indicators to users in plain, accessible Filipino/Taglish to combat social engineering manipulation.

### 2. Blockchain Technology
- **Consortium Fraud Ledger (L2 Rollup)**: A decentralized, immutable blacklist registry storing verified fraudulent telephone numbers, mule GCash/Maya accounts, and malicious phishing domains.
- **Tamper-Proof Auditability**: Because records are cryptographically signed and stored across peer validator nodes (telecoms, fintechs, and community authorities), corrupt actors and cybercrime syndicates cannot bribe or alter their fraud records.
- **Community Evidence Staking**: Victims submit cryptographic proof (SHA-256 evidence hashes) tied directly to case files compatible with Philippine law enforcement (PNP-ACG / CICC).
